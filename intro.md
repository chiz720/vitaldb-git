# Quantitative Anesthesia
## From Numbers to the Operating Room

> *"In God we trust; all others must bring data."* — W. Edwards Deming

---

Welcome. This book is written by an anesthesiologist, for anesthesiologists.

Every case you do generates continuous streams of data — haemodynamic, respiratory, pharmacological, neurological. The monitor alarms when a number crosses a threshold, but it cannot tell you *why*, *for how long*, *how often*, or *what it predicts*. That is what this book teaches.

The goal is not to turn you into a statistician. It is to make you a more precise clinician — one who can read a methodology section critically, interpret a confidence interval correctly, fit a dose-response curve to real patient data, and understand what a machine learning model is actually doing.

### What You Will Learn

The book is organised as a progressive curriculum:

| Part | Theme | Key Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **I — Foundations** | The language of data | Variable types, descriptive statistics, visualisation |
| **II — Inference** | Making decisions from data | Probability, hypothesis testing, confidence intervals |
| **III — Modelling** | Understanding relationships | Regression, survival analysis, prediction |
| **IV — Physiology Made Quantitative** | The three Ps | Cardiovascular, respiratory, pharmacology, EEG |
| **V — Advanced Data Science** | The OR meets the algorithm | Machine learning, time series, signal processing, causal inference |

### Primary Dataset

Every chapter draws on **[VitalDB](https://vitaldb.net)** — 6,388 real surgical cases from Seoul National University Hospital, including continuous haemodynamic monitoring, ventilator waveforms, EEG-based depth of anaesthesia, TCI infusion pump records, and serial laboratory data.

All analyses are fully reproducible. Every figure in this book can be regenerated by running the corresponding notebook.

### How to Navigate

- **Trainees:** Read sequentially from Chapter 1. Complete the VitalDB exercises in each chapter.
- **Consultants:** Jump to Part IV (physiology) or any chapter relevant to your practice.
- **Researchers:** Part V and Chapter 20 (full workflow) are the core.
- **Data scientists new to anaesthesia:** Read Part IV before touching the machine learning chapters. The physiology is the ground truth.
